# Usage Policy

This project is a personal learning project. It is a technical utility that converts media files on your own computer. It does not host, store or share any content.

## Your responsibility

- Only convert files that you own, that are in the public domain, or that you have permission to use.
- Audio and video are usually protected by copyright. Converting or sharing copyrighted material without the copyright holder's permission may be illegal where you live. Whether a particular use counts as "fair use" or "fair dealing" depends on your country, and you are responsible for deciding that.
- This tool does not give you any rights to the content you process.

## Privacy

Files are processed on the machine that runs the server and are deleted when each request finishes. The app has no accounts and does not collect personal information.
